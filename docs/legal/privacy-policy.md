# OSPR Onboarding Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 10, 2026  
**Effective Date:** August 10, 2026

## 1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how **OSPR Onboarding** (the "Bot," "Service," "we," "us," or "our"), operated by **OSPR**, handles information collected or processed through the Service.

OSPR Onboarding is a Discord bot designed to assist Discord communities with member onboarding, verification, questionnaires, rules acceptance, introductions, and related administrative processes.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Service when it is installed or used in any Discord server, including servers operated by organizations, businesses, communities, or individuals.

### Server-Specific Responsibility

OSPR Onboarding may be used by multiple independent Discord servers. Each server may configure its own onboarding requirements and may determine what information it asks members to provide.

The **owner or administrators of the applicable Discord server ("Server Owner")** are responsible for determining the purposes and requirements of their server's onboarding process, including what information they request from members and how that information is used within their community.

OSPR operates the technical Service that facilitates these processes. Depending on the particular data and processing activity, OSPR may act as a service provider, processor, or independent controller under applicable privacy laws.

This Privacy Policy does not replace or supersede the privacy policy, rules, or other policies of the Discord server in which the Service is being used.

## 2. Information We Collect

OSPR Onboarding is designed to collect and retain only information reasonably necessary to operate the onboarding functionality.

Depending on how a particular Discord server configures the Service, information may include:

### Discord Account Information

The Service may receive information made available through Discord's API, which may include:

- Discord User ID
- Discord username and display name
- Discord server membership information
- Discord roles and permissions relevant to the onboarding process
- Other Discord account or server information necessary to perform requested bot functionality

We do not intentionally collect Discord account credentials, passwords, authentication tokens, or payment information.

### Onboarding Responses

If a server uses questionnaires or other onboarding forms, the Service may process the responses submitted by a Discord member.

The nature and sensitivity of these responses depend on the questions configured by the applicable Server Owner.

Server Owners should not use the Service to request sensitive personal information unless they have an appropriate legal basis and the Service is specifically configured and authorized to handle that information.

### Introduction and Other Discord Messages

If configured by a Server Owner, the Service may process messages or other content submitted as part of an onboarding or introduction process, including messages posted in a designated introduction channel.

The Service is not intended to indiscriminately collect or retain all messages sent throughout a Discord server.

### Onboarding and Administrative Status

The Service may store information necessary to determine whether onboarding requirements have been completed, such as:

- Rules acceptance status
- Questionnaire completion status
- Introduction completion status
- Verification status
- Relevant timestamps
- Discord roles or permissions associated with onboarding
- Server-specific configuration and administrative settings

### Technical and Operational Information

The Service may generate or process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the Service.

This may include:

- Request timestamps
- Error and diagnostic information
- Service logs
- Discord API response information
- Server and bot configuration identifiers
- Security and abuse-prevention information

**The Discord bot does not ordinarily receive a user's IP address through the Discord API.** If OSPR directly operates additional web pages, forms, APIs, or other services that collect IP addresses or other technical information, those activities may be subject to additional disclosures applicable to those services.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed through OSPR Onboarding may be used for the following purposes:

1. **Onboarding and Verification**  
   To determine whether a member has completed the onboarding requirements configured by the Server Owner.

2. **Access and Role Management**  
   To assist with assigning, removing, or maintaining Discord roles and access based on onboarding status.

3. **Community Administration**  
   To provide Server Owners and authorized moderators with information necessary to administer their community and manage the onboarding process.

4. **Security and Abuse Prevention**  
   To detect or investigate spam, abuse, unauthorized access, impersonation, malicious activity, or attempts to circumvent onboarding requirements.

5. **Service Operation and Maintenance**  
   To operate, monitor, troubleshoot, maintain, and improve the reliability and functionality of OSPR Onboarding.

6. **Legal Compliance**  
   To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or valid governmental requests.

7. **Protection of Rights and Safety**  
   To protect the rights, property, safety, and security of OSPR, our users, Discord communities, or other persons where reasonably necessary.

We do not sell personal information collected through OSPR Onboarding.

## 4. How Information Is Shared

Information processed through the Service may be disclosed or made available in the following circumstances.

### The Applicable Discord Server

Information submitted as part of onboarding may be accessible to the Server Owner and authorized administrators or moderators, depending on how the server and bot are configured.

For example, onboarding responses may be made available to authorized server administrators for the purpose of reviewing membership applications or determining whether onboarding requirements have been satisfied.

OSPR does not control how a Server Owner or its authorized moderators subsequently use information made available to them through Discord or the Service.

### Discord

OSPR Onboarding operates through Discord and its APIs. Information transmitted to or received from Discord is also subject to Discord's policies and terms.

### Service Providers

OSPR may use third-party infrastructure, hosting, database, security, monitoring, analytics, or other technology providers to operate the Service.

These providers may process information on OSPR's behalf and are expected to access information only as necessary to provide the relevant service.

Depending on the Service's deployment and configuration, infrastructure may include cloud-hosting and database services such as **Cloudflare Workers, Cloudflare Pages, and Cloudflare D1**.

The specific infrastructure used by the Service may change over time.

### Aggregated or De-Identified Information

OSPR may use aggregated or appropriately de-identified information for purposes such as understanding Service usage, identifying operational problems, measuring performance, and improving the Service.

We will not intentionally use de-identified information to identify individual users.

### Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

- Comply with applicable law or legal process;
- Respond to a valid subpoena, court order, or governmental request;
- Protect the security or integrity of the Service;
- Investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents; or
- Protect the rights, property, or safety of OSPR, users, Discord communities, or others.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

OSPR takes reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information processed through the Service against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Depending on the Service's current architecture, information may be stored using cloud infrastructure, including services such as Cloudflare Workers, Pages, and D1.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Accordingly, while we take reasonable precautions to protect information, **we cannot guarantee absolute security**.

Access to stored information is restricted based on operational requirements and, where appropriate, authentication and authorization controls.

## 6. Data Retention

OSPR retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide, secure, maintain, and improve the Service, or as otherwise required or permitted by applicable law.

Retention periods may vary depending on:

- The type of information;
- The functionality enabled by a particular Discord server;
- The Server Owner's configuration;
- Security and abuse-prevention requirements;
- Operational and technical requirements; and
- Legal or regulatory obligations.

Server Owners may establish additional retention requirements for information they collect through their own onboarding processes.

Where information is no longer reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, OSPR may delete, aggregate, or de-identify it, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply to your information, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

These rights may include:

- **Access:** Request information about personal data held about you.
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate information.
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
- **Restriction or Objection:** Request that certain processing activities be restricted or object to certain processing.
- **Data Portability:** Where applicable, request a copy of certain information in a portable format.
- **Withdrawal of Consent:** Where processing is based on consent, withdraw that consent where applicable.

Because OSPR Onboarding may process information at the direction of an individual Discord server, some requests may need to be directed to the **Server Owner or administrators of the Discord server where the information was submitted**.

For example, if a questionnaire response was collected for the purposes of deciding whether someone may join a particular Discord community, the Server Owner may be the appropriate party to contact regarding that information.

You may also contact OSPR using the information in the **Contact Us** section below. We will evaluate requests and, where appropriate, coordinate with the applicable Server Owner.

We may need to verify a request before fulfilling it in order to protect the privacy and security of users.
