## Decisions Made

- Gate architecture: independent nullable step timestamps + one `evaluateGate` function, rather than a linear state-machine enum — steps can complete in any order (chosen because "member types their own message" in #introductions can't be sequenced by the bot anyway)
- Onboarding state persists in SQLite (`better-sqlite3`); no ORM
- Experience-level questionnaire answer is stored only, not mapped to a Discord role
- Rejoin auto-restores `verified` unless a moderator hold is in place; accepted trade-off is documented in the spec
- No kick/ban automation anywhere in this bot — reminders cap at two and then stop
